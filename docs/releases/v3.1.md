ConcertCutter 3.1 improves playback and mouse-based cut adjustments, and fixes video fade synchronization.

- The playhead can be dragged in the detailed view. The pointer becomes an open hand on hover and a closed hand while dragging.
- Boundaries can be dragged across their full height, with a horizontal resize cursor on hover. If a boundary and the playhead overlap, the middle moves playback while the handles move the cut.
- Playing the orange margins preserves the selected track and its boundaries. Playback tracking keeps the viewport and edited segment consistent.
- In one-image-per-track mode, the image fade starts with the audio fade. Matching durations, such as 2 seconds, produce synchronized transitions when tracks are long enough.
- Short tracks are included in chapter timing and no longer shorten image fades across the entire concert.

Regression tests cover playback tracking, selection and transitions encoded with ffmpeg.

Download **ConcertCutter.exe** from this release's assets. The executable is self-contained for Windows 10 and 11.
