ConcertCutter 4.0.0 introduces a complete video montage editor directly within the export flow.

- A timeline lets you arrange audio tracks and images independently using drag and drop.
- Each clip can be moved, trimmed, and positioned precisely, with zoom, preview, and playhead controls.
- Undo and redo history makes montage adjustments safer.
- Track titles can be shown or hidden and positioned at the top, center, or bottom of the frame.
- Transition fade duration is configurable, while gaps without an image render against a black background.
- The new export chooser clearly separates audio outputs from video montage creation.

This version can be installed directly from ConcertCutter 3.4 through automatic updates.

Download **ConcertCutter.exe** and **SHA256SUMS.txt** from this release's assets.
